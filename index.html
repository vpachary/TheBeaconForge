How We Work

A direct, three-step delivery process

No endless committee meetings. We follow a clear, collaborative sprint from concept to deployment.

Step 1

Scope & Discovery

We review your commercial goals, target audience, and content requirements over a focused 30-minute kickoff or written brief. We agree on the site map, package scope, and timeline before anything starts.

Outcome: Clear proposal and locked production schedule.
Step 2

Design & Code

I build interactive preview pages on private staging URLs. You review the real typography, page rhythm, and mobile behavior directly on your phone and desktop rather than static slide decks.

Outcome: Staging site ready for your structured review.
Step 3

Refine & Deploy

We run structured revision rounds to fine-tune copy and visual details. Once approved, I connect your custom domain, provision SSL certificates, verify email forms, and hand over all credentials.

Outcome: Live flagship site on fast edge hosting.

All direct communications use authentic sender identity (hello@thebeaconforge.com), clear subject headers, and accurate commercial information. We do not engage in automated cold spam blasts.

You maintain full rights to inspect, update, or permanently delete any stored correspondence records. Contact hello@thebeaconforge.com at any time to request data removal.
