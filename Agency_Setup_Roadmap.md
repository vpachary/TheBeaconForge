# Agency Setup Roadmap — Exact Order

Do these in order. Each step depends on the one before. Total setup cost: under ₹15,000. Time: ~1 week part-time.

---

## PHASE 1 — Decide before you buy (Day 0)

Before spending a rupee, lock these. Buying first = rework later.

1. **Agency name** — short, easy to spell, `.com` available. Avoid your own name (looks freelancer, not agency). Avoid "India" in the name. Good pattern: `[word]studio`, `[word]labs`, `[word]web`, `[niche]sites`.
2. **Niche** — recommended: US home-services (HVAC / plumbing / roofing). Pick this first because it shapes your name, email, and website copy.
3. **Positioning line** — "We help [niche] in the US get more bookings with a modern website + AI that answers every lead 24/7."

> Do NOT skip this. If you buy a domain before picking a niche, the name often won't fit and you restart.

---

## PHASE 2 — Domain (Day 1)

**Buy the domain FIRST — everything else (email, website) hangs off it.**

1. Check `.com` availability for your agency name at **Namecheap** or **Cloudflare Registrar** (Cloudflare = cheapest, no upsells).
2. Buy the **`.com` only**. Ignore `.net`, `.info`, `.online` upsells. `.com` = trust with US clients.
3. Cost: ~₹900–1,200/year (~$11).
4. Turn ON auto-renew and free WHOIS privacy (Cloudflare/Namecheap include it free).

> Why .com and not .in: US/AU clients trust `.com`. A `.in` domain silently signals "offshore, cheaper" — the opposite of your positioning.

---

## PHASE 3 — Business email (Day 1, right after domain)

**Never use gmail.com for outreach.** `you@youragency.com` is the single biggest trust signal in a cold email. `yourname@gmail.com` gets ignored or spam-filtered.

1. Set up **Google Workspace** (₹136/user/mo, "Business Starter") OR **Zoho Mail** (free tier for 1 user, cheaper).
   - Recommended: Google Workspace — best deliverability, works with every tool.
2. Create your main address: `hello@youragency.com` or `yourname@youragency.com`.
3. **Set up email authentication** (critical for cold email — skip this and you land in spam):
   - **SPF** record (tells servers you're allowed to send)
   - **DKIM** record (signs your emails)
   - **DMARC** record (ties it together)
   - Google Workspace gives you these to paste into your domain's DNS. Do all three.
4. **Warm up the inbox for 2–3 weeks before mass outreach** — send a few normal emails daily, reply to some. New domains that blast 50 cold emails on day 1 get blacklisted.

> For scale later: buy 2–3 *extra* domains (e.g. `getyouragency.com`) with their own inboxes just for cold outreach, so if one gets flagged your main domain stays clean. Not needed on day 1.

---

## PHASE 4 — Your own website (Day 2–4)

This is your proof you can build. It must be fast, clean, mobile-perfect. If your own site is bad, no one buys a site from you.

1. **Pick your builder** — use the same tool you'll sell clients (so you get fast at it):
   - **Framer** — fastest, most modern-looking, great for a one-pager. Recommended.
   - **Webflow** — more powerful, steeper learning curve.
   - **WordPress + Elementor** — most flexible, what most local-biz clients expect.
2. **Build ONE page** with these sections in order:
   - Headline = your positioning line + the outcome
   - Who you help (the niche) + the problem
   - Your 3 packages (Launch / Growth / Dominate)
   - Before/after of a site you built (see Phase 5)
   - A short Loom video or photo (humans buy from humans)
   - One clear button: "Book a free 15-min audit"
3. **Connect your domain** to the site.
4. **Add a booking link** — free **Calendly** or **Cal.com** — so prospects self-book calls.
5. Cost: builder free–$15/mo. Domain already bought.

> Keep it ONE page. A 5-page site for yourself is procrastination. One sharp page that loads in 1 second beats a big slow one.

---

## PHASE 5 — Portfolio / proof (Day 3–5, overlaps Phase 4)

You can't sell with an empty portfolio. Seed it before outreach.

1. Build **2–3 free or ₹-token demo sites** for real local businesses — friends, family businesses, a local shop, or a US business you rebuild speculatively.
2. Capture **before/after screenshots** (old ugly site → your new one).
3. Get **one written testimonial** even if the work was free.
4. Put these on your website's proof section.

> A "spec redesign" (you rebuild a real business's site without being asked, then show them) doubles as a portfolio piece AND an outreach hook.

---

## PHASE 6 — Payments setup (Day 4–6)

Set this up BEFORE your first client says yes, so you can invoice instantly.

1. **Open a current account** in your business name (Udyam + PAN + Aadhaar to start).
2. **Open Wise Business + Payoneer** — both. Clients pay in USD, converts to INR into your account.
   - Wise = best rates. Payoneer = some US clients prefer it.
3. **Set up invoicing** — Wise/Payoneer both invoice for free. Or Zoho Invoice (free).
4. **Confirm you can pull an FIRC / FIRA** (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) for each payment — this is your legal FEMA proof the money is an export earning. Wise/Payoneer + your bank provide it.
5. **Book a 30-min CA consult** now (₹2,000–5,000) to set up GST + LUT + bookkeeping correctly from day one. Do NOT wait for "₹40 lakh" — that limit is a myth. The real trigger is the ₹20 lakh GST threshold, and clean setup from payment #1 saves a mess later.

---

## PHASE 7 — Tools + lead engine (Day 5–7)

Now build the machine that finds clients.

1. **Lead scraping** — Apify / Google Places API / a Maps scraper. Pull businesses in your niche + city with no or bad websites.
2. **Cold email tool** — Instantly or Smartlead (or manual Gmail to start). Connect your outreach inbox (the warmed-up one).
3. **Loom** (free) — for your 2-minute audit videos, your #1 reply-getter.
4. **Notion or Trello** (free) — track leads and client projects.
5. **AI chat receptionist** — Chatbase / Voiceflow — set up a demo one so you can show clients live.

---

## PHASE 8 — First outreach (Day 7 onward)

1. Scrape 100 leads in your niche.
2. Filter to no-website / broken-website businesses.
3. Record a Loom audit for each of the first 20.
4. Send 20 personalized, **CAN-SPAM-compliant** cold emails (real mailing address + unsubscribe line, honest subject).
5. **Email + LinkedIn only. NEVER cold-call or text US numbers** (TCPA — up to $1,500 per message).
6. Follow up 3–4 times over 2 weeks. Most replies come from follow-ups.
7. Repeat daily. Volume in the first 90 days decides whether this works.

## OUTREACH COMPLIANCE — Read Before Contacting Anyone

Two different US laws. Don't mix them up.

### CAN-SPAM (covers EMAIL only)

Cold-emailing a US business is **legal** — no prior consent needed, even if you scraped the email from their Google Maps listing. You stay compliant if every email has these 4 things:

1. **Real sender** — real name + real `you@youragency.com`. No fake/misleading "From".
2. **Honest subject line** — must match the content. No "Re: your invoice" tricks.
3. **Real physical postal address** in the footer (home, a trusted contact's address, or a virtual mailbox — see privacy note below).
4. **Working unsubscribe** — a way to opt out, honored within 10 days. Never email them again after.

Paste-ready footer:

```
[Your Agency Name]
[Real street address], [City], India
Don't want these emails? Reply "unsubscribe" and I'll remove you.
```

> Do NOT put scraped/no-consent emails into Brevo, Mailchimp, or similar bulk tools — their terms ban cold lists and you'll get banned. Use a cold-email tool (Instantly/Smartlead) or manual Gmail instead. A tool does not make you compliant — the 4 items above do.

### TCPA (covers CALLS and TEXTS)

CAN-SPAM does NOT cover phone. Calls/texts fall under TCPA, which is stricter and expensive:

- Cold-calling a Do-Not-Call-registered number or a cell = **$500–$1,500 per call**.
- Cold-texting a scraped number = near-automatic violation, same fines.
- From India you cannot tell which scraped numbers are cells or on the DNC list.

**Rule: never cold-call or cold-text a number scraped from Google Maps.** Only call after they reply and agree to a call (then you have consent).

### What to do with scraped Google Maps data

| You scraped | Action | Legal? |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Cold email (with the 4 CAN-SPAM rules) | Yes |
| Email | Add to bulk marketing tool (Brevo/Mailchimp) | No — terms ban it |
| Phone | Cold call | Risky — TCPA, avoid |
| Phone | Cold text/SMS | No — TCPA, don't |
| Phone | Call after they agreed | Yes — you have consent |

**Safe playbook:** scrape Maps → open with **email + LinkedIn + contact form** → they reply → *then* call if needed.

### Privacy note (address without exposing your home)

The postal address is required for cold email, but it does NOT have to be your home:

- LinkedIn messages, website contact forms, and social DMs need NO postal address — start there for ₹0.
- For cold email: use a trusted contact's address (free) or a virtual mailbox (US ~$10–30/mo, India ~₹400–800/mo) once you have your first client.
- Don't mention India in the email body — lead with the outcome. Location isn't the headline.
- Never fake being a US company you're not — that's fraud. A virtual address is legal and normal; a fake identity is not.

> Other markets: Canada (CASL) and EU/UK (GDPR/PECR) require consent for cold email — harder. Stick to US (and Australia) at the start. This is general information, not legal advice; confirm with a professional before a large-scale calling operation.

## DECISIONS FROM OUR Q&A (things you asked → what to apply)

Everything you asked during planning, with the answer you'll actually use.

**Q: What is CAN-SPAM?**
US email law. Cold B2B email is legal — no consent needed — IF: real sender, honest subject, real physical address in footer, working unsubscribe honored ≤10 days. A tool doesn't make you compliant; those 4 items do. → Apply: use the footer on every cold email.

**Q: Brevo (from Namecheap) — is it for CAN-SPAM / cold email?**
No. Brevo/Mailchimp ban scraped/cold lists (account gets banned) and use shared IPs (spam). Use Instantly/Smartlead or manual Gmail for cold outreach. → Apply: keep Brevo only for opted-in contacts/clients later, NOT cold Maps leads.

**Q: Free LLC / US LLC?**
Skip for the first 6–12 months. You can invoice + get paid via Wise/Payoneer without one. An LLC adds cost + mandatory US tax filings (Form 5472/1120; $25k penalty per miss for non-residents). → Apply: operate as your Indian business now; revisit LLC only at steady US revenue, with a CA.

**Q: I don't want to share my home address (fear of racism / looking like a scam).**
Legit. CAN-SPAM needs a real postal address for EMAIL only — not your home. LinkedIn/contact-forms/DMs need NO address. → Apply: start on LinkedIn + forms (₹0); for cold email use a trusted contact's address (free) or a virtual mailbox once earning. Trust comes from your site + Loom + portfolio, not from hiding location. Never fake a US identity (fraud).

**Q: Can I call a US number I got from Google Maps? Is that CAN-SPAM?**
No — calling isn't CAN-SPAM, it's TCPA, and it's dangerous ($500–$1,500 per call/text; can't verify DNC/cell from India). → Apply: NEVER cold-call or cold-text scraped numbers. Email + LinkedIn to open; call only after they agree.

**Q: Can I email a business email from their Maps listing?**
Yes — legal under CAN-SPAM with the 4 rules, even though it's scraped. → Apply: email is your main channel.

**Q: Where should I host client sites?**
Static local-biz sites → Cloudflare Pages / Netlify (free, fast CDN). WordPress/backend → Cloudways/Hostinger. → Apply: host under YOUR account (leverage), pick US/AU data center, default to Framer as builder (hosting built-in, low maintenance).

**Q: Backend needed → hosting, else Netlify/Cloudflare — right?**
Correct split. But most local-biz sites need almost no backend (forms, booking, chat are all embeds/APIs). → Apply: default to Cloudflare/Netlify; use a real host only for WordPress/e-commerce. Justify the retainer by what you MANAGE, not by hosting cost.

**Q: Self-hosted SuiteCRM from GitHub + the 4 CAN-SPAM fixes — good to go?**
No, premature. Don't self-host a CRM before you have leads, and never send cold email from self-hosted SMTP (spam). → Apply: Notion/Trello for pipeline + Instantly/Smartlead for sending now; consider SuiteCRM only at real volume.

**Q: How to hand over the website without trouble later?**
Run Model A (build + maintain, retainer). Client owns a dedicated project Gmail (domain/Google Business/Analytics under it). → Apply: final 50% clears BEFORE handover; ownership transfers only on full payment; get written "approved & live" reply (chargeback protection); your leverage is hosting-under-your-account + contract, NOT the Gmail login. Full clauses + checklist in `Full_Agency_Guide.md` §13.

**Q: Client gives me a new Gmail for the project — good?**
Yes, clean setup (client owns their identity, no "hostage" accusations). → Apply: client owns the Gmail with their recovery phone; you operate it with the password in your manager; don't rely on it as leverage (they can reset it).

**Q: Which client documents are mandatory?**
Only 4 to start: contract, proposal, invoice + cold email/Loom to get leads. → Apply: use the Tier 1 pack in `client-docs/`. Don't build a client portal early (time-sink). Add Tier 2/3 docs as you land clients. See `client-docs/00_MASTER_CHECKLIST.md`.

**Q: What's the real problem with the VM / shell?**
An interrupted app update left the VM component unverified ("SDK version not verified"). → Apply: reboot the machine first; reinstall the app only if reboot fails. It only blocks the Word .docx + git clone; all your files are saved as markdown and work fine.
