### **$1,000** one-time investment

### **What is Included**
- **Up to 5 Custom-Designed Pages:** e.g., Home, About, Services, Case Studies / Gallery, Contact.
- **Bespoke UI/UX Design:** Tailored typography, color palette, and conversion layout (no off-the-shelf, bloated themes).

- **WhatsApp Click-to-Chat Integration:** Instant 1-click messaging for rapid lead conversion.

5. **Approval & Final Payment:** Final 50% balance settled prior to live DNS cutover and asset handover.
